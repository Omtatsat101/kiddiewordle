Contact

We read every email. Response time is usually a day or two.

The fastest way to reach us

Email riketpatel@hariomtatsatinvestments.com with "KiddieWordle" in the subject line. We get to it within a couple of business days, usually faster.

What to include

  • Bug reports — tell us what you were doing, what device + browser, what happened.
  • Word suggestions or word-list issues — let us know the specific word + difficulty level it appeared at (or should appear at).
  • Language requests — tell us which language and roughly how many speakers / readers in your circle would use it. Volume helps us prioritize.
  • Accessibility issues — if anything in the game is hard for a child with low vision, dyslexia, motor differences, or any other access need, please write in. We treat these as high-priority.
  • Press or partnership — we're a small family operation but happy to talk to schools, libraries, education writers, and aligned non-profits.

What we won't answer

  • SEO outreach offering "guest posts" or backlinks — please don't.
  • App-development pitches — we're browser-first by design.
  • Questions answered on our FAQ page — check there first.
